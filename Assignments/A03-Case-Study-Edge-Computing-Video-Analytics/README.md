## A03: Smart City Traffic Monitoring Case Study
- Problem: The city of Liverpool faced a projected influx of 30,000 daily pedestrians, requiring a non-intrusive way to collect granular mobility data for sustainable urban planning.
- Approach: I analyzed a case study on intelligent visual sensors using edge computing to process video locally, focusing on multi-modal detection of pedestrians and vehicles.
- Results: The analysis highlighted how real-time edge processing can balance privacy with the need for data-driven city infrastructure improvements.
