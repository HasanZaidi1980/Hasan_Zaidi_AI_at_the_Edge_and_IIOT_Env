## A01: Understanding Edge AI Environments
- Problem: The shift from cloud to "Edge Intelligence" requires developers to understand a complex ecosystem of tools to minimize latency and energy costs.
- Approach: I conducted a comprehensive review of foundational tools like VS Code, GitHub Copilot, and TensorFlow Lite to evaluate their roles in optimizing AI for local hardware.
- Results: I developed a technical report that serves as a roadmap for selecting the right IDEs and frameworks for professional Edge AI development.
